**Audit scope:** Article 174 only; review of the existing canonical records, verification ledger, timeline note, local PDF inventory, and official consolidated edition. No canonical record or verification status was changed.

## Current evidence position

It shows Section 1 is only a short-title clause and Section 8 says Article 174 “shall be substituted.” The official consolidated text independently annotates the Section 8 substitution as effective from 18 June 1951.

The local official Constitution snapshot is `raw/constitution/Constitution_of_India_2024.pdf`, as on **2026-05-01**, SHA-256 `4c8e93689ec245e26db08e22b5ff3ad645c98736f0e5b7c8b6638acd7861f85b`. Article 174 and its footnote (PDF p. 111) show the present consolidated wording and identify only the First Amendment Act, section 8, with effect from 18 June 1951. The same edition is available from the [Legislative Department official PDF](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf); its Preface confirms the edition’s stated amendment scope and annotation convention, and its Article 174 page contains the Section 8 note. The original Constitution is locally available at `raw/sources/Constitution_of_India_English_1949.pdf`: Article 174 at PDF p. 106, printed p. 89, and Article 394 at PDF p. 250. Article 394 supports the original version’s commencement on 26 January 1950.

No First Amendment Gazette PDF or other Gazette-named file is present in the current `raw/` inventory. The source ledger records that a previously considered December 2010 weekly scan was not the 1951 First Amendment Gazette and is not evidence for it. The online [Central Secretariat Library Gazette of India page](https://csl.nic.in/index.php/page/innerpage/gazette-of-india.php) says digitized Gazette material from 1950–2002 is available through the Department of Publication’s search service; the exact 1951 issue was not retrieved or inspected in this audit.

## Answers and decision

**Section 8 effective date:** The 1951-06-18 date is already stated directly in the official consolidated Article 174 footnote. Section 1 does not defer commencement or authorize a commencement notification. No separate appointment notification is missing for this Act.

**Assent and publication:** No locally available primary Gazette issue or explicit primary assent record was found. The local Act and online official copy show `[18th June,1951.]` below the title, while secondary institutional sources identify assent/publication on that date. These date-provenance gaps should remain explicit; neither should be conflated with the effective date.

**Later history:** The official 2026 edition’s Preface provides the completeness statement missing from the selected local amendment corpus: it says all amendments through the 106th Act have been incorporated and that footnotes identify the Acts making amendments. Article 174’s page lists the First Amendment Act, section 8, w.e.f. 18 June 1951, and no later Article 174 change. Together, those official pages support the Article 174 amendment history through **2026-05-01**. They do not support claims after that date.

**Evidence coverage:** On the combined source record, defensible cutoffs are **1951-06-17** for the original version and **2026-05-01** for the replacement version. The first is the last inclusive day before the original record’s exclusive end; the second is the date of the official consolidated edition. These are evidence-coverage cutoffs, not new legal interval endpoints. Recording either cutoff would not change the current `partially_verified` status and would not make System C eligible to retrieve the records.

**Decision:** The Article 174 text and its legal interval through the 2026-05-01 snapshot are supportable with the local primary Act/original Constitution sources plus the official comprehensive consolidated edition. The missing Gazette facsimile and explicit assent record prevent direct primary-source verification of publication and assent claims, but do not appear to block the Article-specific effective intervals or bounded evidence coverage. Do not promote either canonical status without an authorized status review under the project’s verification rule. If the rule requires assent/publication to be independently proved even when they are not legal interval boundaries, the exact missing source is the **Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, Issue No. 21, dated 18 June 1951**, plus an explicit primary assent record if the Gazette does not state assent.
